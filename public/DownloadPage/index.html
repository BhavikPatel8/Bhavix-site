How to Install

1
Tap the Download WebLock button above — the APK file will start downloading.
2
Open your notification panel or go to your Downloads folder and tap the downloaded APK file.
3
If prompted, tap "Allow from this source" to permit installing apps from outside the Play Store.
4
Tap Install on the install screen and wait for the process to complete.
5
Tap Open — WebLock is ready to use!
If Install Problem

Disable Google Play Protect

Play Protect sometimes blocks apps from outside the Play Store. If WebLock isn't installing, follow these steps — tap any step to see a screenshot guide.

1
Open Google Play Store and tap your Profile icon in the top-right corner.
View
2
Scroll down and tap Play Protect from the menu.
View
3
Tap the Settings (⚙) icon in the top-right corner of the Play Protect screen.
View
4
Toggle off Scan apps with Play Protect — a confirmation dialog will appear.
View
5
Tap "Turn off" in the dialog — do NOT tap Pause.
View
6
Both toggles go grey — Play Protect disabled. Now try installing WebLock again!
View
